Markag — Libya's marketplace

Markag is a mobile marketplace app for Libya. Sell an item in a minute, and bid or buy directly from sellers in every Libyan city.

  • Auctions — the winner gets a notification and has 48 hours to pay.
  • Buy It Now and offers — buy at the listed price or send the seller an offer.
  • Buyer protection — payment is held by Markag and released to the seller only after you confirm delivery, or automatically after 7 days if you don't open a dispute.
  • Chat with sellers inside the app without sharing your phone number.
  • Delivery prices per city set by each seller, shown before you buy.
  • Wallet for purchases; sellers' earnings are paid out to their bank account.

Sign in with email or with your Google account. We don't sell your data and show no third-party ad network ads. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
